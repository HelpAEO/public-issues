# HelpAEO — Public Issues

This is where you tell us what's broken and what you'd like to see next in
[helpaeo.com](https://helpaeo.com).

You don't need a technical background to post here. If something didn't work,
that's worth reporting — even if you're not sure why.

**[→ Report a bug](../../issues/new/choose)**  ·  **[→ Suggest an idea](../../issues/new/choose)**  ·  **[→ Browse what's already reported](../../issues)**

---

## Before you post

Have a quick look through [open issues](../../issues) to see if someone already
reported it. If they have, add a 👍 or a comment with your own details instead of
opening a new one — it helps us see what's affecting the most people.

---

## Reporting a bug

Open a new issue and answer these five things. Copy the template below straight
into the issue body if that's easier.

1. **Where did you find it?** — the page URL, or the part of the app (e.g. "the
   scan results page", "the sign-up form", "the PDF export").
2. **What happened?** — one or two sentences in your own words.
3. **What did you expect to happen instead?**
4. **Steps to reproduce** — the clicks you made, in order, so we can see it too.
5. **Screenshots** — drag any image straight into the issue box. A screenshot of
   the whole browser window, including the address bar, is the single most useful
   thing you can give us.

Also helpful, if you know it: your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox…), whether
you're on desktop or mobile, and roughly when it happened.

## Suggesting an idea

Ideas are just as welcome as bugs — a missing feature, a confusing label, a
report you wish we produced, a tool you'd want us to integrate with.

Open an issue and tell us:

- **What you'd like** — the feature or change.
- **Why** — what you're trying to get done, and what you do today instead.

Don't worry about whether it's feasible. Describing the problem you're hitting is
more useful to us than a fully-formed solution.

---

## What happens next

We read everything that comes in. You'll usually get a reply within a few working
days, and we'll close the issue with a note when the fix ships or when we decide
not to do it (and why).

We can't promise every suggestion gets built, but every one gets read.

---

## Please don't post here

- **Anything private** — passwords, API keys, billing details, or personal data
  belonging to you or your customers. This repository is public and everything in
  it is visible to anyone. Blur or crop screenshots before uploading.
- **Security vulnerabilities** — email **hello@helpaeo.com** instead, so we can
  fix it before it's public.
- **Account, billing, or sales questions** — email **hello@helpaeo.com**.
